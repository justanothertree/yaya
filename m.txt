feat(games): the minions room is the playground

"yes merge the playground into the minions page." It was a tile of its own, which meant
the room holding your creatures and the room where they run about were two places — and
the whole point of moving everything behind one door was that those are one thing.

The playground is the page now, above the roster, because it is the part that MOVES:
arriving somewhere your creatures are already running about is a different welcome from
arriving at a list with a button marked play. The ways in are down to four — the park, a
scrap, make a minion, make a map — because the fifth is what you are looking at.

`?play=playground` still means something and what it means is this room. A link that
stopped working is the one thing SECTION_ALIASES exists to prevent, one level down.

The keyboard guard became a RULE rather than a list, which is what its own note asks for
after the last time: a room in here takes the keys when there is a game ON its page. That
is the scrap, and the minions room now — and only when there is a creature to play, or an
empty roster would eat the arrow keys for a platformer that is not on screen.

**And the audit cried wolf about a whole game**, which was worth fixing rather than
noting. It treated an ancestor as owning its own width only when you could SCROLL it —
`auto` or `scroll` — and missed the other way a container owns its width: a camera. The
playground's stage clips a world wider than the screen, so its ledges and treats sit
hundreds of pixels past the right edge on purpose, and cannot be seen, scrolled to, or
made to overflow the page. Flagged, that is two findings on a main room at every run, and
this file's whole doctrine is that a checker nobody runs twice is no checker. `hidden`
counts now. It costs nothing, because `sideways` is the backstop and does not go through
that rule: if anything ever does push the document wider than the window, that number
says so whatever is clipping what.

Verified in the pane: the room shows the ways in, then the playground, then the roster,
and ArrowRight walks a creature from 0.14 to 0.5 of the stage. `?play=playground` lands
here with the platformer on it. Re-audited at 375 across home, paint, games, instrument,
visualiser and this room — every one clean on all four counts, and the two false
positives gone. 608 tests.

Not verified: whether one page holding a platformer and a roster is too much at once.
That is a thing to feel rather than measure.
