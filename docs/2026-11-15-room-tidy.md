# The room, tidied around the part menu — 15 Nov 2026

Evan: "look for anything in the room still scattered or hard to find now that the part menu
exists, and fold it into the menu or the play bar. Before moving anything, list what moves and
where in the PR."

## What moves, and where

Nothing is removed. Every control below can still be reached; four things gain a home beside the
things they belong with.

| What                                | Where it is now                                        | Where it goes                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Your keys' volume                   | only in 🎚 Mix, among the parts                        | also at the top of ⚙ Sound, with the rest of your keys' sound. It stays in Mix.               |
| 🥁 Beats (its button)               | the tools row: 🥁 Beats · 🎚 Mix · 🎤 Voice · 📁 Songs | under the parts, beside ✎ New part and 🎤 Add lyrics: every way to add a part, in one row      |
| Recordings playing in the loop      | only inside 🎤 Voice                                   | a row each in the parts list, with its mute. Its name opens 🎤 Voice, where its controls stay. |
| A part's bars (which bars it plays) | the tiles on its row, explained only by tooltips       | also in the part menu's More: which bars it plays, how the tiles work, and Play in every bar   |

Why each one:

- **Your keys' volume.** Everything else about how your keys sound is behind ⚙ Sound: the
  arpeggio, the effects, the rigs. The one thing that was not was how loud they are.
- **🥁 Beats.** A part could be added from three places: ⏺ Record and ⧉ Capture with the
  transport, ✎ New part under the parts, and 🥁 Beats among the tools below that. Record and
  Capture stay with Play, because they are things you do while it plays. Beats joins New part. The
  panel it opens is unchanged, and on an empty song "🥁 Pick a style, a beat or some chords" still
  opens it.
- **Recordings.** A voice take in the loop is part of the song you hear, and the parts list is where
  the song is listed. It was missing from it: muting a take meant opening 🎤 Voice or 🎚 Mix.
- **A part's bars.** The tiles on a part's row are the arrangement: tap one to pick it up, tap
  another to move it, tap it again to silence it. That was only ever said in tooltips, and a phone
  has none. The part menu is where a phone goes for a part.

## Not moved, and why

- **↶ ↷ Undo and redo** stay above the parts they undo, beside the ruler. On a phone the transport's
  two rows are already full.
- **⏹ Panic** stays on the play bar, where a stuck note is noticed.
- **🎚 Mix** keeps every volume, part volumes included: it is the one place to balance the song.
- **🎤 Voice's door to the lyrics** stays for anybody who learned it there.
