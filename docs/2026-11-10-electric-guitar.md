# An electric guitar — 10 Nov 2026

Evan, from the loop: "more instruments". The Strings family had a harp, a koto, a sitar and a
banjo, and no guitar, which is the instrument most people would look for there first.

**🤘 Electric guitar** (clean) is now in the Strings family, which makes eight: one row at 1440.

## What makes it a guitar and not another pluck

- **It holds.** Harp, Koto, Banjo and Pluck all die away to nothing (a sustain of 0). A pickup and
  an amp keep part of the note ringing, so this one holds 0.22 of its level and a chord can sit
  under a song. Measured 1.2 s after the attack, Guitar reads 0.048 and Harp 0.005.
- **A chorus:** two copies of the note 8 cents apart, the effect nearly every clean tone is played
  through.
- **A mid-bright filter**, falling from 3.4 kHz to 1.3 kHz, so it doesn't become the Supersaw.
- A nylon or acoustic guitar would be nearly identical to Harp in this synth, so it was not added.

## Measured

- **Loudness against Keys**, with the real synth: attack 0.225 vs Keys 0.232 at C4, and 0.20–0.23
  from E3 to C5. Its first level, 0.42, read 0.155 (3.7 dB under Keys), so it was raised to 0.62.
  The peak is 0.61 against Keys' 0.45, which is what a saw does, and within the basses' range.
- **Tests:**
  - It holds where every other plucked string has a sustain of 0. Setting its sustain to 0 turns
    this red.
  - It differs from every Strings voice on at least two things a listener hears: the same check
    the basses use, now shared.
  - It travels through a song file as itself.
- **375:** the Strings family shows eight tiles, Guitar 77 × 68. `mobileAudit` reports zero after
  reporting a planted fault first.
- **Keyboard:** Enter on the tile selects it, and the menu button reads "🤘 Electric guitar".
- **Console:** no errors.
